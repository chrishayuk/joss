# Scans of the original sources

Page images from the scans of RAND's JOSS papers at Bitsavers
(<https://bitsavers.org/pdf/rand/joss/>), reduced in size. They are here so
that the reconstruction can be checked against what was actually typed. Page
numbers are those of the PDF.

| Folder | Source |
|--------|--------|
| `p-3131-1965/` | J. C. Shaw, "JOSS: Examples of the Use of an Experimental On-Line Computing Service", P-3131, April 1965. Pages 3 to 10: eight pages typed at a JOSS console on 28 April 1965, in two colours. |
| `p-3149-1965/` | J. C. Shaw, "JOSS: Experience with an Experimental Computing Service for Users at Remote Typewriter Consoles", P-3149, May 1965. Figure 1 (p. 6), figure 2 (p. 8) and the one-page summary of the language (p. 11). |
| `p-2922-1964/` | J. C. Shaw, "JOSS: A Designer's View of an Experimental On-Line Computing System", P-2922, August 1964. The console (p. 9), the keyboard (p. 11) and figures 3a to 3i (pp. 16-24). |
| `rm-4162-1964/` | C. L. Baker, "JOSS: Scenario of a Filmed Report", RM-4162-PR, June 1964. The scenario (pp. 10-18), the facsimiles of typewriter output in its Appendix A (pp. 22-24, 27-29, 31-32) and the list of words (p. 51). |
| `program-notes/` | "JOSS Program Notes and Tests", Shaw's working papers: the typed summary of the language with his amendments (pp. 1-3), a test strike of the type ball (p. 11), and his notes of 11-19-64 on the log routine and 10-10-64 on the error line (pp. 13-14). |
| `listing-1965/` | The JOHNNIAC assembly listing headed "JOSS 666, JCS 1-9-65": its first page, and the error directory and message texts (pp. 37-42). |

Where to look:

- A page as JOSS left it, the user in green and JOSS in black:
  `p-3131-1965/page-06.jpg`. It is also the best single example of an error
  put right: "Error at step 1.4:  e is undefined.", `Set e=.00005.`, `Go.`
- The quadratic equation and its nine mistakes: `p-3149-1965/page-08.jpg`.
- The summary this reconstruction takes as the definition of the language:
  `p-3149-1965/page-11.jpg`.
- The three places where what JOSS typed differs from this reconstruction in
  the last digit: `p-2922-1964/page-17.jpg` (`log(2) = .69314718`),
  `rm-4162-1964/page-11.jpg` (the long expression), `rm-4162-1964/page-23.jpg`
  (the row for 72).
- The messages as the program holds them, five characters to a word in
  octal, with the words printed beside them in capitals:
  `listing-1965/page-038.jpg` onward.
